3.
At the beginning of the university year a student must chose some courses.
The available budget is 10000 units and three types of courses are available:
 a. Cost: 1000 units, credit points: 5, individual study required: 80 hours;
  b. Cost: 800 units, credit points: 3, individual study hours required: 40 hours;
  c. Cost: 1500 units, credit points: 8, individual study hours required: 100 hours.
   Use a genetic algorithm to help the student choose a combination of course (how many of each type) such that it fits within the available budget,
    maximizes the average number of credits and the average individual study hours is at most 70.
     Note: average number of credits per course and average number of study hours per course are computed as weighted average

 --genotype: each chromosome is represented as a vector of 3 integers,each integer
 represents the nr of courses of type:a,b,c.
 --phenotype is derived from genotype, where each chromosome's integer values correspond to
 the nr of courses for each type
 --solution space: all possible combinations where the sum of the elements doesn't exceed the budget
 and each element represents the nr of courses for each type.
 --model:use GA to find the combination of the courses that maximize the average nr of credits while
 the average individual study hours < 70
 --objective function:maximize the average nr of credits while penalizing
 the sol that > 70 (constraint)
--multipoint crossover operator(r_multipunct):exchange genetic material between 2 parents
--integer mutation operator using drift (m_int_fluaj) : allow small changes in the nr of courses taken for each type
--parent selection:roulette wheel : select parents based on their fitness
--next generation(elitist selection) : the best chromosomes are preserved in the next generation
--termination condition: nr of iteration and the algorithm return the final population
