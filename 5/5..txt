In order to supply areas in distress with basic supplies, a humanitarian convoy is formed as a train with n cars.
 The train will go through each area and unload supplies.
  supplies are packed in m special containers (m>>n).
  The mass for each container is known (can be read from a text file).
   Use a genetic algorithm to find a way to distribute the containers into cars so that the mas is distributed
   as evenly as possible. There is no upper mass limit for cars.

   --genotype: represent each solution(distribution of containers into cars) as a list of length m
   where each element represents the index of the car to which the corresponding container is assigned
   --phenotype: the distribution of the containers
   --solution space: each element can take values from 0 to n-1, where n is the nr of cars
   --model: a permutation problem, find the best permutation of containers into cars to minimize the mass imbalance
   --objective function: variance of the mass distribution among cars
   -- crossoer(CX)(cicluri) to generate offspring sol from selected parents
   --mutation: random swappping of containers between cars or randomly reassigning containers to different cars
   
    --selection(s_ruleta_SUS,d_FPS_ss):
--parent selection: roulette wheel selection with sigma where the selected individual is proportional to its fitness. 
the fitness is calculating using the fitness function and sigma scaling for uniform selection probability. 
--selection for next generation: elitist selection where the best individual is copied to the next generation.
 the best solution is found. 
 
 --the termination condition could be the max nr of generations reached . the algorithm stops when the satisfactory solution is found.
