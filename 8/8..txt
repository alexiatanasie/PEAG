A university organizes a scientific expedition by sea to visit some virgin islands in X archipelago, starting from one port of the archipelago and returning to the same port. The ship cannot be supplied during voyage. The available resources on board allow visits to only n islands from 1000 islands that form the archipelago n<<1000) and a total travel distance of k km. use a genetic algorithm to chose the islands to be visited and the order to visit them. It is preferred to visit islands as far away as possible. The distances between islands (including the home port) are given as a weight matrix in a text file, where D(i,j) = distance between islands i and j.
